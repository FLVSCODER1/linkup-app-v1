# LinkUp v0.7.0-beta.5

## Community school requests

- Replaces the unsupported-school signup dead end with a **Request my school** action.
- Adds a mobile-first `/request-school` form for official school details, email domain, website, and an optional activities calendar.
- Validates requests server-side and rejects personal email providers or unsafe website URLs.
- Deduplicates nominations by school domain and requester email so repeated demand increases a trustworthy request count instead of creating paperwork spam.
- Keeps requests and requester emails server-only; community submissions never activate a school automatically.
- Adds an orange **School requests** admin tool and protected review API.
- Approving a request atomically activates the district, school, and email-domain mapping in the existing directory.
- Reviewers can mark a domain as school-specific or shared across its district.
- Adds empty, loading, error, duplicate, success, approval, and rejection states.

## Security

- Firestore clients cannot read or write `schoolRequests` or their requester records.
- Only full admins or active `school_directory_admin` role holders can inspect and review requests.
- Approval checks for conflicting school IDs and domain mappings before changing the directory.
- A honeypot and idempotent requester records reduce basic automated and repeat submissions.

## Review checklist

- Submit an unsupported school from `/signup`.
- Confirm a repeat submission from the same email is counted only once.
- Confirm the request appears under `/admin/school-requests`.
- Verify approval creates active district, school, and domain records together.
- Verify rejected requests disappear from the pending queue.
- Confirm non-admin users cannot access the admin queue or server-only Firestore records.
